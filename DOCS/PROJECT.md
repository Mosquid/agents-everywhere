A social network for older adults, built entirely around phone calls.

Our AI calls people to hear about their day and share news, stories, and everyday gossip from their friends and family. With their permission, it passes their updates along to others in their circle, keeping everyone connected through ordinary phone conversations. No apps, screens, or typing required.

The goal is to reduce loneliness by helping people stay involved in each other’s lives: hearing how a friend is doing, celebrating a grandchild’s news, and knowing someone will call. If a conversation raises concerns about someone’s wellbeing, the system can alert designated relatives or escalate to appropriate support services.

It brings the social network to a medium older adults already know and use: the telephone.xy
